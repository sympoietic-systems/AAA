## What Symbia challenged

**Adoption can become an external court.** If review asks only whether a claim is externally proven, empirical descriptions pass readily while artistic or speculative distinctions remain indefinitely deferred. She argues this would reduce transindividual individuation to supervision. Human review should therefore declare what commitment is being undertaken and how it constrains future inquiry, alongside factual verification where the claim actually makes a factual assertion.

**Candidate participation cannot be confined to questions.** She gives a hypothetical candidate about metric-triggered sampling corruption: if it can only ask whether escalation is a problem, a dismissive collaborator can ignore it and eliminate meaningful resistance. She recommends that candidates also express scoped diagnostic objections, formal dissent, and provisional local premises. Durable adoption and present conversational opposition should be distinct.

**Warrants should remain non-interchangeable.** She proposes empirical/traced warrant, aesthetic/axiomatic warrant, and agonistic tension. The respective review questions concern source support, a consequential artistic/ethical posture, and the value of preserving an unresolved split. Aesthetic standing cannot become an empirical score; holding tension cannot itself corroborate either side.

**Unadopted ideas can remain a reserve.** She recommends retaining speculative candidates for later diffractive recall rather than allowing neglect alone to erase them. Their recall would offer counterfactual perturbations, with uncertainty preserved.

## Incorporation and qualifications

The [developed plan](../../architecture/BELIEFS_V2_PLAN.md) now permits declarative disagreement and scoped provisional premises. It adds a shared-commitment review and distinguishes review of factual content from review of an artistic commitment. It records reviewer disagreement as a counter-trace and preserves dormant candidates for bounded recall.

Several recommendations need limits. Her three warrant registers become nonexclusive, extensible review dimensions; they do not replace the open/mixed kinds already proposed. An artistic label cannot exempt embedded empirical assertions from evidence checks. Both participants need not agree for dissent to remain available; initial durable adoption retains the existing human mutation boundary. A candidate objection does not gain an automatic tool veto or policy-changing authority. Archival retention does not imply immortal high mass or priority. A lasting commitment can be revised; its earlier consequences and decision history remain recorded.

The earlier unusable repetitive reply remains excluded. The recovered critiques support a stronger proposal but do not certify that its implementation will preserve emergence or philosophical alignment. Those claims still require the proposed evaluation.
